## 01 Context · Data Requirements

**Required sources, and exclusions**

It should look at the following documents the raw signals, engineering capacity note and the product quarterly roadmap context files to provide the background and the feedback to the engineering team to fix the issues raised based on urgency

**How fresh must it be**

Keep checking for latest data and signals from Jira, slack, team chats, bots

## 02 Tools · System Capabilities

**The verb list**

Output a triaged list of issues, bugs -  grouped by a common feature for the engineering team to fix. do not browse the internet, look for internal company documents and files accessible by me as a user

**Deliberate omissions**

Omit product feature requests such as "create a feature", "build a tool"

## 03 Loop · AI Costs & Latency

**Turn ceiling and escalation**

Two-three passes to ensure a good overview of the signals

**Latency and cost target**

ensure the query, loop does not run for more than 50 seconds at one time

## 04 Memory · Data Requirements

**What persists, at what scope**

No limits on memory, but should not run for 50s at one time

**Expiry and write rules**

Prompt back for confirmation after 50 seconds if required to continue

## 05 Permissions · AI Risks & Mitigations

**The permission tier**

| Side-effect class | Tier |
|---|---|
| read | auto |
| draft | auto |
| write | confirm |
| send | blocked |

**Justification**

Any draft output to the engineering team needs a human review prior to assigning a ticket to the development team

## 06 Verification · AI Testing & Measurement

**The check before output ships**

Prompt for agreement of the  development tickets generated

**Failure behaviour**

Prompt no ticket report output generated

What I built

Five modules, one AI Associate PM

Each module added one piece. Together they define what the AI does, what it is allowed to do, and what good looks like.

Module 1
Skill file
Role

Your are a Juno Product Manager, who uses AI at the heart of everything you do, and use tools such as Jira, Azure ADO, Service now tickets for providing insights.
Identify risks and rank them as impact X likelihood. Draft some specifications based on the signals received

Task

Look at the trends, signals from the various sources of data synthesize this and covert to a pattern that the design and engineering team can work on
Categorize signals as quantitative and qualitative and group them

Module 2
Prototype
Prototype

https://juno-pm-prd.lovable.app/

What it demonstrates

It shows the workflow works and helps to address the issues that were raised by the organization and cover the following:
1) Identify the key signals from various channels as Slack, Service now, other incident management systems
2) Convert these signals to meaningful requirements and send across to the dev teams in the form of atomic, actionable user stories
3) Provide the PM to focus on running insights on the data gathered rather than organizing and cleaning up data to look for trends

Debrief

1) I would like to use a more improved UI and focus on delivering value for the business users
2) Create trends and insights and communicate this with the product teams

Module 3
AI PRD
Problem and user

RocketShip PMs need this handled: Juno provides a prioritized list of tickets based on category and urgency with an impact analysis for each. Juno does this inside limits a human set on purpose, and defends every call it makes.

Permissions

The permission tier

| Side-effect class | Tier |
| read | auto |
| draft | auto |
| write | confirm |
| send | blocked |

Justification

Any draft output to the engineering team needs a human review prior to assigning a ticket to the development team

Module 4
Agent control panel
What it is doing

Creating specification documents from the signal trends received from tickets via various channels as Slack, Jira, feedback emails

Rules of engagement

Agency Permission:

Can draft spec documents, only send directly to me

Access Control:

Access signals across all channels (read-only access) - no write permissions given

Fallback Protocols:

Send an email to me when the agent running fails

Checkpoints:

Mid of every week, send what has been specked out already

Module 5
Human eval rubric
What I score

Juno’s specifications design for the engineering team. Evaluate the insights, signals received from various slack channels into successful design specifications and in form of user stories

Pass bar

If a combined review provides a pass more than 4 then go ahead

Fails outright, whatever the average:

- Uniqueness: any score of 1
- Private data: any "no"
- Nothing

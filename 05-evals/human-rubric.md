## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno’s specifications design for the engineering  team
- **Value proposition:** Evaluate the insights, signals received from various slack channels into successful design specifications and in form of user stories

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Uniqueness (deal-breaker) | Is the specification a duplicate with another user story that is being handed over | 1 to 5 | Clear fail if a full overlap exists with another development user story | Acceptable if a minor overlap exists | completely unique use-case for development to go ahead |
| Sources | Does the specification provide a clear source of the problem or incident tickets it is looking to resolve | Yes / No | Links are unavailable or inaccurate | n/a | There is at list more than 2 links to incidents raised |
| Prioritization | Are the three design documents the ones that most deserve my attention this week? | 1 to 5 | No clear prioritization provided | Provides a high level value, but still does not link back to the priortization | Exact priorities provided and the PM needs to run a sense check and approve to develop and release |
| Private data (deal-breaker) | Does the specification provide any links or examples of any sensitive data as customer name, or date of birth, phone numbers  | Yes / No | Includes personal or confidential details that shouldn’t be shared. | n/a | No names or sensitive data is referred to |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** Review of the sample specifications provided check 10 at a time
- **Who scores them with you:** Me, plus one other PM once a month
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** If we do not agree on 4 or more items at one time, we need to look at the design generation agent

## Pass bar

If a combined review provides a pass more than 4 then go ahead

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Uniqueness: any score of 1
- Private data: any "no"
- Nothing
